## First comment (maker's intro — le plus important)

> Hey Product Hunt 👋
>
> I'm Théo, a live-event technician. For years I re-did wireless mic frequency plans
> in a rush, double-checked power calculations on the back of a napkin, and built
> tech sheets in Word at midnight.
>
> Pro software exists — but it's expensive, complex, and often English-only, made
> for engineers, not for someone who has to move fast between two setups.
>
> So I built the tools I wish I'd had — simple, in the browser, nothing to install:
>
> • Speed RF — a reliable wireless mic/IEM frequency plan in seconds
> • RF Shot — advanced coordination for big shows (100+ frequencies, intermodulation up to 7th order)
> • AV Calc — 71 pro calculators for sound, light, video, power, rigging (free)
> • Fiche Tech Creator — clean technical rider/spec sheets, exportable to PDF (free)
>
> One-time purchase, lifetime license, no subscription.
>
> ⚠️ Heads-up: it's currently in **French** (built for the French live-events scene).
> An English version may come later. I'd genuinely love your feedback — and I'm here
> all day to answer questions 🙏
